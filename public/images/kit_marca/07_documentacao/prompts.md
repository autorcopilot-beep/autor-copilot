# Prompts das ilustrações

Método: ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. Imagens novas, geradas com IA; não são pinturas de um artista humano. A referência orienta a paleta e o acabamento.

## romance

Create one finished square editorial watercolor illustration for a premium reading and writing app. Subject: Two folded ivory love letters tied with a sage green silk ribbon, one small white camellia with dark green leaves. Quiet intimate literary romance, no hearts. Style: refined naturalistic handmade watercolor and dry brush on cold-pressed paper, pigment granulation, restrained delicate detail, organic uneven edges, understated mature design like traditional book arts. Palette strictly ivory #F7F5EC, sage #9AAE99, dark forest green #254D3D, muted olive accents. Composition one cohesive isolated vignette centered occupying 75% of canvas, generous clean margin, plain warm ivory background #F7F5EC. No black background, no lettering, no logos, no text, no cartoon, no 3D, no glossy effects, no ornamental clutter. Fine nuanced brushwork. Output high quality 1536x1536 or larger.
