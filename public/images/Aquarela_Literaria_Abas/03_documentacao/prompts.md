# Direção das sete ilustrações

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. A referência do kit inicial orientou apenas paleta, textura e acabamento; a arte não é feita à mão por artista humano.

## Dashboard

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square tab illustration for a premium literary reading and writing app. Main subject: A calm editorial desk still life: three overlapping blank ivory dashboard cards arranged asymmetrically, one with simple unlabeled sage bar shapes, one with a graceful unlabeled curve and one with three small green circular markers, an olive sprig partially behind. Concept: overview/dashboard. Absolutely no text or numbers. Match the accompanying visual style reference in spirit: traditional, believable artisan watercolor, delicate dry brush, cold-pressed paper grain, minor pigment variations, subtle dark green outlines, no synthetic glow. Exact palette inspired by reference: warm ivory paper #F7F5EC, forest green #254D3D, sage #9AAE99, olive #7C8767. Isolated single vignette, occupies 65–78% of square frame, warm off-white paper background without border, clear legibility as a small navigation illustration. No human-made UI screenshot. Absolutely no text, writing, letters, numerals, glyphs, logos, watermarks or labels anywhere, even on books/paper. No cartoon or 3D, no excessive fantasy effects.
